Our mission

We provide opportunities for girls and women with disabilities to build self-esteem.

Miss Amazing creates spaces where girls and women with disabilities can define their identities and futures on their own terms. We do that three ways.

  • 01

    Personal Development

    Equipping participants with the tools to own their stories and build a plan for their lives.

  • 02

    Friendship & Community

    Creating genuine peer connections, and empowering the families and allies who support them.

  • 03

    Shifting Perceptions

    Engaging communities directly to confront and dismantle the stigma around disability.

Our program

AMPLIFY

We draw inspiration from pageantry to teach vital self-advocacy skills, spark connections, and open up pathways to leadership. AMPLIFY is a one- to two-day event for girls and women with disabilities ages 5 and up — fun, supportive, and with real stakes.

What participants do

  • Interview

    A friendly conversation with judges about her interests and goals — real practice at speaking for herself.

  • Passion Presentation

    Ninety seconds on stage to share a hobby, a talent, or whatever she loves most.

  • Introduction

    An opportunity to stand in front of an audience and speak — for many, a first.

How the day runs

  1. Registration and buddy matching
  2. Orientation — welcome and announcements
  3. Show practice
  4. Interviews with judges
  5. Hair and makeup
  6. The final show, introductions, and awards

Who can take part

Girls and women ages 5 and older who have an IEP, IPP, 504 plan, SSI, or a physician's documentation of disability are eligible.

Age divisions

Rising Stars (ages 5–9) get a simplified, gentler experience. Ages 10 and up participate fully, in categories running from Preteen through Mentor (36+).

What it costs

Nothing! We do host a food drive the weekend of the event though, which our state Queens distribute back into the community.

This is not a beauty pageant.

Miss Amazing does not ask its judges to evaluate participants based on physical appearance.
